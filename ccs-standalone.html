CCS — Co-Created Stories

みんなで つくる にほんごの ものがたり

🎌 What this is

A co-created story: you choose the language, the class chooses the story. Everything appears in Japanese with furigana, and every fact the class decides on gets asked back to them several times (circling) before the story moves on.

1Choose a word pack

Start from a ready-made pack, then edit anything below. Your changes are saved in this browser.

2Story shape

The skeleton the class's decisions get poured into. All four are built the same way underneath — decide, hear it circled back, move on.

3Target grammar

The 2–3 structures you want the class to hear again and again. Put a slot in curly brackets — {もの}, {ばしょ}, {ひと}, {どうし}, {きもち} — and the story fills it in. Write furigana as 学校[がっこう].

4Vocabulary

These are the choices the class picks from. Three or more words per list is plenty — fewer words means more repetition, which is exactly what you want with Years 7–9.
